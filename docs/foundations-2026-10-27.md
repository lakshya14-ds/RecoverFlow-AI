# Foundation repair and verification — 2026-10-27

The local pilot now reports cash receipts separately from credit notes. An invoice's opening amount plus signed ledger entries determines outstanding; each new receipt or credit records how much of the disputed balance it settles. Reversals reference their original entry and restore its dispute allocation. A legacy dispute larger than the remaining balance is shown as a staff reconciliation case with no eligible undisputed amount. Staff can review the source and record the corrected open dispute.

Money columns formerly stored as floating point use exact decimal text in SQLite. The migration rebuilds affected legacy tables, preserves indexes and foreign keys, validates references, and records a schema revision. Run it against a backup and in a maintenance window. A PostgreSQL upgrade path is implemented but was not validated with a PostgreSQL instance because this verification used SQLite. The receivables ledger is an immutable adjustment log, not a double-entry accounting ledger.

The investigator compares item-level invoice and purchase-order prices and can assess delivery shortages, returns, and documented scheme discounts. Staff-approved SKU aliases can join equivalent item names. Ambiguous items, missing tax or freight allocation, overlapping claims, contradictory sources, and invalid arithmetic pause contact for staff review. A staff decision is linked to evidence and the document digest, so new evidence makes an older decision stale.

Controlled-agent proposals and delegated negotiation offers are tied to a case fingerprint. Outbound wording is checked against server-calculated balances, permitted installment amounts, waiver language, destinations, and untrusted instructions. These are conservative controls; multilingual and obfuscated attacks still require ongoing adversarial evaluation. The legacy graph, intake path, and controlled-agent path now checkpoint one shared case state after their principal transitions. This provides a common revision and audit trail, while their specialized processes remain separate.

## Remaining limits

Historical entries without allocation metadata cannot be retrospectively assigned to disputed lines without staff review. Text-only PDFs and the seeded synthetic example are stronger than unstructured, scanned, multilingual business documents; broader labeled field trials are needed. The project still needs production authentication, backups, observability, and real provider checks before live business use. Payments and messaging in the hackathon demo remain **SIMULATED** or sandboxed.

## P0 completion — 2026-10-28

Accounting and currency: every receivable path (imports, intake confirmation, staff entries, reconciliation approval, simulated webhook, reversals, reports, drafts, negotiation) uses integer minor units for ledger math and `Decimal` for monetary values. CSV import, PDF pipeline, and Shopify schedule import no longer pass floats into money fields. Cash receipts, credit notes, reversals, and other adjustments stay distinct in storage, API, reports, and UI. `post_entry` rejects overpayment, excess reversals, currency mismatches, and silent rounding (more than two decimals). A credit is never cash collected or a verified payment.

Dispute allocation: each payment/credit records its disputed portion under an explicit policy (`undisputed_first`, `disputed_first`, `undisputed`, `disputed`); reversals restore the original allocation exactly. Every posting writes a `ledger_allocation` audit event (kind, policy, disputed delta, original entry), and reusing a `source`/`reference` pair is idempotent only for the identical entry. Legacy entries without allocation metadata are reported as unallocated adjustments and force staff reconciliation; the case view stays open with `reconciliation_error`, zero eligible undisputed amount, and stage `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

Canonical lifecycle (`app/services/case_lifecycle.py`): the single stage set is `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `CONTACT_PAUSED`, `DISPUTED`, `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, `PARTIALLY_RESOLVED`, `RESOLVED_PAID`, `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED`, reconciled with legacy `Invoice.status` (`paid`, `adjusted`, `PARTIALLY_RESOLVED`, `disputed`, `partially_matched`, `pending`) by `settle_invoice_status`. Only ledger and staff-review events move the legacy status; draft, approval, and sandbox events never close (or reopen) a case. Drafts and approvals carry the case fingerprint, so new evidence or payments make older approvals stale (HTTP 409). A case closed with credits is labeled `adjusted` / `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED`, never paid.

Evidence trust states: `alleged` → `extracted` → `verified` → `accepted`, plus `rejected`. Customer statements and model extractions enter as allegations/extractions; staff verify a source or accept a claim for financial reliance, each decision recording actor, timestamp, amount, currency, and reason. Migration `20261028_trust_states_v2` backfills old `verified=True` rows to `verified` only — never `accepted` — and old `verified=False` rows to `alleged`. Ledger adjustments always use their own authorized posting path (staff entry, admin reversal, staff-confirmed receipt, approved reconciliation, verified webhook); accepting evidence alone never moves money. The case API and UI show trust state, decider, reason, exact source, and snippet; derived amounts that no longer match the ledger are labeled historical.

Staff-reconciliation procedure: open the flagged case (it stays readable), inspect ledger entries with their allocation and disputed deltas plus the linked evidence and its trust state, then either record the corrected open dispute via Save staff dispute decision (citing the reviewed evidence) or accept/reject individual claims from the evidence cards. Contact, drafts, and payment links stay blocked until the math is consistent.

Honesty notes: the receivables log is an immutable adjustment log, not double-entry accounting. AXIS is a local tax-classification database reader, not an Axis Bank connection. Razorpay payments are **SIMULATED**, messaging is sandbox-only, and the regex/policy guards are a defense layer, not injection immunity.
