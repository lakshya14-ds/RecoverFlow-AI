AI Recovery Autonomy Level

Choose how independently the AI handles customer reminders.

Operating mode: Dispatches strictly to simulated sandbox outbox. Dual-control staff review required before external transmission.

How Autonomy Works For You

  • Review All: Zero customer messages are sent without your explicit sign-off. Best for maintaining personal buyer relationships.
  • Auto-Prepare: The AI extracts deductions, cross-references receiving notes, and prepares 1-click reminders so you don't do data entry.
  • Autonomous Pilot: For high-volume retailers. Prepares polite reminders only for undisputed balances below your safety threshold. Dispatches strictly to simulated sandbox outbox.
